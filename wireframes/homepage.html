Designer Notes

Brand color rationale: Teal (#0D9488) is distinctive in the fact-checking space — most incumbents use navy or orange. It conveys trust and accuracy without feeling corporate or aggressive. Works well at small sizes (badges, logos) and large (hero backgrounds).

Alternatives to consider: If teal feels too "health-app", a deep navy (#1E3A8A) gives more gravitas. If a younger/tech audience is the target, a slate-based palette with a green accent would feel more modern.

Verdict color system: Red/amber/green follows universal traffic-light logic — universally understood without needing to read the label. Outdated (blue) and Satire (purple) are differentiated enough to not conflict with the core three.
