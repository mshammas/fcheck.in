FALSE
"Drinking warm water does not cure viral infections"
The Claim
"Drinking warm water kills viruses and cures infections including COVID."
Circulating on WhatsApp via voice notes since Feb 2026 across South Asia.
Our Verdict FALSE
Warm water has no antiviral properties. While it may soothe a sore throat, it cannot kill viruses or cure infections. Confirmed by WHO, CDC and multiple virologists.
Evidence
Source 1
WHO Statement Feb 2026
"No evidence warm water prevents any viral infection or reduces viral load in the body…"
View source →
Source 2
The Lancet Jan 2026
Review of 14 studies found no antiviral effect from warm water consumption under controlled conditions…
View source →
Source 3
CDC Guidance Ongoing
No recommendation for warm water as treatment for any viral infection. Standard hand hygiene and vaccination remain primary preventive measures…
View source →
Where did this claim come from?
Traced to a voice note on WhatsApp attributed to "a doctor." No such doctor has been identified. A similar claim circulated widely in 2020 during the early COVID-19 pandemic and was debunked then too.
